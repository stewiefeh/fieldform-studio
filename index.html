What we believe

A building should ask for less attention than the life inside it.

The practice

Small on purpose.

The studio

One team, start to finish.

The people who sketch your building also walk the site during framing. No handoffs to a production office, no junior bait-and-switch after the render looks good.

Materials

Honest ones.

Concrete that shows its formwork, timber that darkens with use, brick from the region it sits in. Materials chosen to look better in twenty years, not twenty days.

Energy

Passive first, panels second.

Orientation, massing, and envelope do the heavy lifting. Mechanical systems are sized for the building, not the brochure.

Budgets

Said out loud, early.

Cost is a design input, not a surprise at tender. Every sketch we present carries a square-meter estimate you can check.

How a project runs

Four phases, no mystery.

Listen

Two weeks on your site, your habits, and your budget. We measure how you actually live before we draw anything.

Sketch

Three directions, each with a cost band. You pick one, we push it hard, and nothing is precious at this stage.

Resolve

Permits, consultants, and a drawing set a builder can price without guessing. Weekly cost checks from here on.

Deliver

We stay on site through construction and walk the punch list with you. The project ends when the building works.
