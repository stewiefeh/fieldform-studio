Services

Architecture, interiors, and the homework before both.

We do four kinds of work. Most projects combine the first two. The second two exist because a feasibility study is the cheapest architecture you will ever buy.

01

Architecture

Full service from first sketch to final walkthrough. We carry the project through permitting, pricing, and construction with the same team that designed it.

  • Concept through construction administration
  • Permit and tender drawing sets
  • Weekly site walks during build
  • Cost checks at every phase gate
02

Interior architecture

Rooms, joinery, lighting, and materials designed with the shell, not against it. One palette from front door to roof deck.

  • Custom joinery and stair design
  • Lighting layouts and fixture schedules
  • Material and furniture specification
  • Furniture, art, and styling procurement
03

Feasibility & site studies

A 12-week study that tells you what a site or building can legally, structurally, and financially become before you commit real money to it.

  • Zoning and code capacity analysis
  • Massing options with cost bands
  • Environmental and heritage screens
  • Go or no-go recommendation, in writing
04

Master planning

For campuses, estates, and waterfronts: a 10 to 20 year framework that lets individual buildings change without the whole losing its way.

  • Phasing strategy with early-win projects
  • Move and growth modeling
  • Public realm and landscape framework
  • Design guidelines for future teams
Ways to engage

Three shapes, priced honestly.

Full service

The standard route.

A fixed fee by phase, agreed before we start each one. The default for most houses and small cultural work.

Design direction

Your team, our pen.

Your builder or developer holds the delivery. We set the design and review every drawing set that goes out the door.

Advisory

The second opinion.

A day per month on your project, on retainer. Boards, owners, and project managers use it before big decisions.

Questions we get

Answered plainly.

Two numbers, kept separate. Our fee for a full-service house typically lands between 8 and 12 percent of construction cost. Construction itself currently runs $2,800 to $4,500 per square meter for the standard of work we put our name on. We will not know your number until the sketch phase, and we will tell you so at the first meeting.

A house: design 4 to 6 months, permits 2 to 4, construction 10 to 14. A cultural building: two to three years end to end. Anyone promising faster is planning to discover the delay in your change orders.

We are licensed in Oregon and Washington, and partner with local architects elsewhere. Ashgrove House and the Hudson Valley bench prove the model travels. Travel costs are priced into the fee, never discovered later.

Yes, that is the design direction model. One honest caveat: projects where a different team carries the drawings lose detail quality, so we price a review retainer to protect the work. Most clients take it.

That is what the feasibility study is for. Twelve weeks, one number you can take to a lender, and no obligation to continue with us afterward. We say that last part in the contract, not just on the phone.
